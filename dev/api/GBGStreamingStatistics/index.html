GBGStreamingStatistics.GBGStreamingStatistics — Module
GBGStreamingStatistics

A library for online nonparametric statistics, estimating densities, conditional expectations and functionals of these from a stream of samples: each sample is observed once, folded into a fixed-size summary, and discarded. It runs on CPUs and GPUs.

The summary records per bin the count and the power sums of its samples about the bin center. It is estimand-agnostic: every reported quantity is a post-hoc smoothing functional of it, and no stationarity, Markov, or parametric structure is a premise.

It offers these operation families:

  • Summaries — Histogram1D / Histogram2D, filled by observe! / observe_pairs!, added by merge! and, with floating-point counts (count_type = Float64), reweighted by scale! for mixtures weighted after accumulation (hyperstatistics) and exponential forgetting.
  • Smoothing kernels.
  • Marginal density — density, optionally from a parametric start.
  • Conditional expectations — conditional_expectation, conditional_mean, conditional_variance, conditional_pushforward_density.
  • Conditional and transition densities — conditional_density / transition_density.
  • Expectations by quadrature over the power sums — expectation, central_moment, pushforward_density.
  • Bandwidth selection — bandwidth.
  • HDF5 persistence — write_histogram / read_histogram (needs HDF5).
