Regression

The conditional mean conditional_mean $\equiv m(x)$ and conditional variance conditional_variance $\equiv \sigma^2(x)$ (§4.2) — the estimand-first reference module.

GBGStreamingStatistics.Regression.conditional_mean — Method
conditional_mean(summary::Histogram2D, x; h)   # ≡ m(x)

Estimand. the conditional expectation $m(x) = \mathbb{E}[Y \mid X = x] = \int y\, f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)\,\mathrm{d}y$ — the $L^2(\sigma(X))$ projection of the response $Y$ onto the conditioning variable $X$, i.e. the regression function.

Estimator (local-linear; Fan & Gijbels). From the joint summary (a Histogram2D of $(X, Y)$) form the kernel-weighted sums $T_\ell(x) = \sum_i K_h(X_i-x)(X_i-x)^\ell$ and $P_\ell(x) = \sum_i K_h(X_i-x)(X_i-x)^\ell Y_i$ — recovered from the per-X-column counts and response totals $\sum Y$ (themselves exact, from the per-cell power-sums) — and solve the local normal equations:

\[\hat m_h(x) = \frac{T_2 P_0 - T_1 P_1}{T_0 T_2 - T_1^{2}}.\]

Local-linear is the default — it removes the design-density bias $2 m'(x) f'(x)/f(x)$ of the Nadaraya–Watson reduction $\hat m_h = P_0/T_0$. The bandwidth $h$ is supplied (the response-aware local plug-in $h^\star(x)$ is forthcoming, §4.5); a vector of query points returns a vector. Consistency (docs/FORMALIZATION.md): $\operatorname{bias}\hat m_h(x) \approx \tfrac12 h^2 \mu_2(K)\, m''(x)$, $\operatorname{Var}\hat m_h(x) \approx R(K)\,\sigma^2(x)/(n\,h\,f_X(x))$.

Reference: J. Fan & I. Gijbels (1996), Local Polynomial Modelling and Its Applications, Chapman & Hall.

GBGStreamingStatistics.Regression.conditional_variance — Method
conditional_variance(summary::Histogram2D, x; h)   # ≡ σ²(x)

Estimand. the conditional variance $\sigma^2(x) = \operatorname{Var}(Y \mid X = x) = \mathbb{E}[Y^2 \mid X = x] - m(x)^2$, with $m(x)$ the conditional_mean.

Estimator. the same local-linear fit applied to the response $Y^2$ (its per-X-column totals $\sum Y^2$, exact from the per-cell power-sums), combined as $\widehat{\mathbb{E}}[Y^2\mid x] - \hat m_h(x)^2$ (Fan & Yao 1998) and clamped to be non-negative. For heavy-tailed channels (e.g. $(\Delta X)^6$) a one-step robust local M-estimator (Fan & Jiang 2000) replaces the least-squares fit (forthcoming).

Reference: J. Fan & Q. Yao (1998), Efficient estimation of conditional variance functions in stochastic regression, Biometrika 85(3).
