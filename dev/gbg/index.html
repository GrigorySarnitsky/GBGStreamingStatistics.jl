What GBGStreamingStatistics has switched off

Each entry names a rule this package does not hold itself to, and why. A rule listed here is read as the entry states it.

  • RULE.DOC.COORD_FREE — No structured space to state a map intrinsically on: every variate is a scalar on an interval, and the package carries no vector or tensor notation at all (invariant 2; the \mathbf and \partial_i house guards are deliberately absent here). The rule's differential-geometry reader would have nothing to judge, and without the switch every review reports that nobody judges it. The owner, 2026-10-03. (the whole package)
