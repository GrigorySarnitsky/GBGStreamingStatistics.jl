Formalization

The typed statements behind the prose. Each operation gets one entry stating its estimand, its estimator, and the consistency claim — the bias and variance with every normalization constant pinned — each linked to the test that verifies the convergence rate numerically. The chain is prose ↔ formalization ↔ test: the prose (docstrings, ARCHITECTURE.md) agrees with the typed statement, the statement is verified against the code, and the linked test enforces it.

A nontrivial claim with no entry here, or an entry with no test link, is a recorded coverage gap (CLAUDE.md, Definition of done).

Throughout, $K$ is the smoothing kernel with $\int K = 1$, $\mu_2(K) = \int u^2 K(u)\,\mathrm du$ its second moment, and $R(K) = \int K^2$ its roughness; $h$ is the bandwidth, $n$ the number of samples, $f_X$ the marginal density of the conditioning variable.

The implemented entries are test-linked; the conditional entry is the format for what lands next.

Per-bin power-sums about the bin center (Histograms, the summaries)

  • Object. per bin, the count and the power-sums about the fixed bin center $S_p = \sum_{i\in\text{bin}}(X_i - c_j)^p$ for $p = 1 \dots$ order (and order-2 bivariate $S_{rs}$ per cell for Histogram2D). Intrinsically finite-sample — no population estimand.

  • Accumulation. an atomic scatter-add (observe! / observe_pairs!): one work-item per sample adds $(X_i - c_j)^p$ to its bin's $S_p$, in Float64. merge! is elementwise addition (same binning $\Rightarrow$ same centers). The local offsets are well-conditioned, so no compensated summation is used.

  • Recovery. the global central moments are the shift formula

    \[\sum_i (X_i - \bar X)^p = \sum_j \sum_{k=0}^{p} \binom{p}{k}\,(c_j - \bar X)^{p-k}\, S_k^{(j)}, \qquad S_0^{(j)} = n_j, \quad \bar X = \tfrac1n \sum_j \bigl(c_j n_j + S_1^{(j)}\bigr),\]

    which is exact (the $S_p$ use the actual $X_i$, not the center) and numerically stable for the even $p$ the consumers need.

  • Test. test/histograms.jl (recovered $M_2 \dots M_6$ match the direct sum to $10^{-8}$ and the true Gaussian moments to sampling error); test/histograms2d.jl (2D marginals + covariance); test/mergeability.jl (merge $=$ together); test/allocations.jl (bounded observe! / merge!); test/gpu/suite.jl (CPU-vs-AMD-GPU parity).

Marginal density (§4.1) — density

  • Estimand. $f_X = \mathrm{d}\mu_X/\mathrm{d}\lambda$, the Lebesgue density of $X$.
  • Estimator. the binned KDE $\hat f_h(x) = \frac{1}{nh}\sum_j n_j K\!\left(\frac{x-c_j}{h}\right)$ ($\int K = 1$, Gaussian $K$) from the bin counts $n_j$ at centers $c_j$, with $h$ from bandwidth (Silverman $h = (4/3)^{1/5}\hat\sigma\,n^{-1/5}$).
  • Consistency claim ($h \to 0$, $nh \to \infty$): $\operatorname{bias}\hat f_h(x) \approx \tfrac12 h^2 \mu_2(K)\, f_X''(x)$ and $\operatorname{Var}\hat f_h(x) \approx R(K)\,f_X(x)/(n h)$; the binning error $O(b^2)$ is dominated by the $O(h^2)$ smoothing bias when $b \ll h$.
  • Test. test/oracles.jl (recovers the $N(0,1)$ pdf to $< 3\times10^{-3}$ over $[-3,3]$, integrates to 1; the Silverman/Scott bandwidths match their formulas).

Local-linear conditional mean (§4.2) — conditional_mean

  • Estimand. $m(x) = \mathbb{E}[Y \mid X = x] = \int y\, f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)\,\mathrm{d}y$, the $L^2(\sigma(X))$ projection of the response onto the conditioning variable.

  • Estimator. the local-linear fit $\hat m_h(x) = (T_2 P_0 - T_1 P_1)/(T_0 T_2 - T_1^2)$ from the kernel-weighted power-sums $T_\ell = \sum_i K_h(X_i-x)(X_i-x)^\ell$ and $P_\ell = \sum_i K_h(X_i-x)(X_i-x)^\ell Y_i$ — recovered from the joint Histogram2D's per-X-column counts and response totals $\sum Y$ (exact from the per-cell power-sums).

  • Consistency claim (interior $x$, $h \to 0$, $nh \to \infty$):

    \[\operatorname{bias}\hat m_h(x) \approx \tfrac12 h^2 \mu_2(K)\, m''(x), \qquad \operatorname{Var}\hat m_h(x) \approx \frac{R(K)\,\sigma^2(x)}{n\,h\,f_X(x)},\]

    giving the optimal rate $\hat m_{h^\star}(x) - m(x) = O_p(n^{-2/5})$ at $h^\star \sim n^{-1/5}$.

  • Test. test/regression.jl — the bivariate-Gaussian oracle $Y = \rho X + \sqrt{1-\rho^2}\, \varepsilon$ (so $\mathbb{E}[Y\mid X=x] = \rho x$): conditional_mean recovers the slope to $\lesssim 2\times10^{-2}$; conditional_variance recovers $1-\rho^2$ to a few percent (the simple $\hat{\mathbb E}[Y^2\mid x]-\hat m^2$ estimator carries the local-linear smoothing bias of the curved $E[Y^2\mid x]$; the Fan–Yao residual refinement is forthcoming). The $n^{-2/5}$ rate test is forthcoming.

Conditional increment moments, drift & diffusion (§4.4) — increment_moment, drift, diffusion

  • Estimand. the conditional increment moment and the Kramers–Moyal coefficient

    \[M_n(x, \tau) = \mathbb{E}\!\left[(X_{t+\tau} - X_t)^n \mid X_t = x\right], \qquad D^{(n)}(x) = \frac{1}{n!}\lim_{\tau\to0} \frac{M_n(x,\tau)}{\tau},\]

    with drift $\equiv D^{(1)}$ and diffusion $\equiv D^{(2)}$. Model-neutral — a conditional expectation of an increment power, no diffusion premise.

  • Estimator. conditioning on $X_t = x$ (so the increment is $\Delta X = Y - x$ with $Y = X_{t+\tau}$), the order-2 joint summary resolves

    \[M_1(x) = \hat m_h(x) - x, \quad M_2(x) = \widehat{\mathbb{E}}[Y^2\mid x] - 2x\,\hat m_h(x) + x^2, \quad \widehat D^{(1)} = \frac{M_1}{\tau}, \quad \widehat D^{(2)} = \frac{M_2}{2\tau},\]

    where $\hat m_h$ and $\widehat{\mathbb{E}}[Y^2\mid x]$ are the local-linear §4.2 fits (exact from the per-cell power-sums). $n \ge 3$ is a recorded coverage gap — it needs order-$n$ per-cell $Y$ power-sums.

  • Consistency claim. $\widehat D^{(1)}$ is consistent for the finite-$\tau$ drift at the conditional_mean rate, with no smoothing bias where $m(x)$ is linear in $x$; $\widehat D^{(2)}$ carries the local-linear bias from the curvature of $\mathbb{E}[Y^2\mid x]$ (the Fan–Yao residual refinement removes it). The finite-$\tau$ coefficients approach $D^{(n)}(x)$ as $\tau\to0$ up to the $O(\tau)$ finite-time bias (the Bell-polynomial correction, forthcoming).

  • Test. test/oracles.jl — the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck oracle (exact stationary lagged pairs from $\mathrm dX = -\theta X\,\mathrm dt + \sigma\,\mathrm dW$): drift recovers $x(e^{-\theta\tau}-1)/\tau$ to $\le 2\times10^{-2}$ and diffusion recovers the closed-form $[s^2(1-e^{-2\theta\tau}) + x^2(e^{-\theta\tau}-1)^2]/(2\tau)$ ($s^2 = \sigma^2/2\theta$) to $8\%$. test/markovinference.jl — the variance decomposition $M_2 = \operatorname{Var}(Y\mid x) + M_1^2$, the $1/(n!\,\tau)$ scaling, the $n\ge3$ and static-lag coverage-gap errors, and mergeability. The $n^{-2/5}$ rate test is forthcoming.
