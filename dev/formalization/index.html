Formalization

The typed statements behind the prose. Each operation gets one entry stating its estimand, its estimator, and the consistency claim — the bias and variance with every normalization constant pinned — each linked to the test that verifies the convergence rate numerically. The chain is prose ↔ formalization ↔ test: the prose (docstrings, ARCHITECTURE.md) agrees with the typed statement, the statement is verified against the code, and the linked test enforces it.

A nontrivial claim with no entry here, or an entry with no test link, is a recorded coverage gap (CLAUDE.md, Definition of done).

Throughout, $K$ is the smoothing kernel with $\int K = 1$, $\mu_2(K) = \int u^2 K(u)\,\mathrm du$ its second moment, and $R(K) = \int K^2$ its roughness; $h$ is the bandwidth, $n$ the number of samples, $f_X$ the marginal density of the conditioning variable.

The single seeded entry below fixes the format. Entries are added as each module is implemented.

Local-linear conditional mean (§4.2) — conditional_mean

  • Estimand. $m(x) = \mathbb{E}[Y \mid X = x] = \int y\, f_{Y\mid X}(y \mid x)\,\mathrm{d}y$, the $L^2(\sigma(X))$ projection of the response onto the conditioning variable.

  • Estimator. the local-linear fit $\hat m_h(x) = (T_2 P_0 - T_1 P_1)/(T_0 T_2 - T_1^2)$ from the kernel-weighted power-sums $T_\ell = \sum_i K_h(X_i-x)(X_i-x)^\ell$ and $P_\ell = \sum_i K_h(X_i-x)(X_i-x)^\ell Y_i$, read from the per-bin power-sums of the summary.

  • Consistency claim (interior $x$, $h \to 0$, $nh \to \infty$):

    \[\operatorname{bias}\hat m_h(x) \approx \tfrac12 h^2 \mu_2(K)\, m''(x), \qquad \operatorname{Var}\hat m_h(x) \approx \frac{R(K)\,\sigma^2(x)}{n\,h\,f_X(x)},\]

    giving the optimal rate $\hat m_{h^\star}(x) - m(x) = O_p(n^{-2/5})$ at $h^\star \sim n^{-1/5}$.

  • Test. (to be linked: test/oracles.jl — Gaussian/OU oracle; a rate test that the error falls as $n^{-2/5}$.)
